# 🕯️ Apothecary's Grimoire

> *A Halloween brewing game. Pick strange ingredients, read the night sky, and fill your grimoire with potions nobody has bottled before.*

---

## What it is

**Apothecary's Grimoire** is a small, cozy dark-fantasy brewing game that runs in the browser. You play an alchemist working late in an old apothecary on All Hallows' Eve. Fifty ingredients line the shelves, a cauldron bubbles over the fire, and somewhere in those jars are 31 potions waiting to be discovered.

Every brew is an experiment. Your ingredients decide how well the potion is *crafted*. The moon, the hour and the calendar decide how the night *blesses* it. Together they set its rarity, from humble Common draughts to the two Heavenly potions few apothecaries ever see.

**At a glance**

| | |
|---|---|
| Genre | Brewing / discovery / collection |
| Platform | Web browser, desktop and mobile |
| Session length | A few minutes per brew, as long as you like overall |
| Goal | Discover all 31 potions and all 18 ingredient reactions |
| Theme | Halloween, cozy dark fantasy |

## How to play

### 1. Choose your ingredients
Pick **five or six** ingredients from the shelves.
- **Desktop:** browse the ingredient column on the left. Filter by essence or search by name.
- **Mobile:** tap the cauldron (or the **Ingredients** button) to open the ingredient drawer.

Hover or tap an ingredient to read its label. You won't know its hidden numbers until you've brewed with it at least once.

> 💡 **The first ingredient you add is your base.** If it matches the brew's leading essence, the whole brew gets steadier.

### 2. Check tonight's omens
Under the cauldron you'll see the current **moon phase**, the **hour**, any **special date**, and what your pot is **seasoned** with. Hover them for their effects. They change in real time, so the same recipe brewed at midnight on a full moon can come out very differently from one brewed on a sunny afternoon.

### 3. Brew
Press **Brew**. The ingredients tumble in, the fire roars, and the brew swirls through its colours before it erupts. Click or tap anywhere to skip the animation.

### 4. Reveal your potion card
Your potion appears as a collectible card showing:
- **Rarity** and its number in the collection (e.g. *No. 21 / 31*)
- **Elixir or poison**, and its leading essence
- **Name and effect**
- **Arcana** (total power), split into **Craft** (from ingredients) and **Omens** (from the night)
- The omens that touched it, and the recipe you used

Open **How the arcana formed** for the full breakdown, and read the hint for how to push it higher.

### 5. Fill your grimoire
- **Your Shelf** keeps your best brews, ranked by arcana. Click one to see its card again.
- **The Grimoire** (book icon) holds every potion you've discovered, grouped by rarity, plus the ingredient reactions you've found and the lore of the craft. Click a discovered potion to view its card.

## How the cauldron judges

**Arcana = Craft + Omens.** Arcana decides rarity.

### Craft: what your ingredients decide
| Factor | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Bane & balm** | Every ingredient carries hidden bane or balm. More bane makes a **poison**, more balm makes an **elixir**. The winning margin, doubled, is the brew's *potency*. |
| **Power** | Every ingredient's power is added together. |
| **Discord** | Ingredients pulling against the brew (a balm in a poison) cost twice their value. Rock Salt halves discord. |
| **Harmony** | Two of one essence +2, three +5, four +9, five +13, six +16. The leading essence also names the potion. |
| **Reactions** | Certain pairs bond (+6) or clash (−6). Spring Water cancels one clash. |
| **Commons** | Honey +4 to elixirs, Vinegar +4 to poisons, Candle Wax +3 to anything. |
| **True base** | First ingredient matches the leading essence: +3. |

### Omens: what the night decides
| Omen | Effect |
|---|---|
| 🌕 **Full moon** | Elixirs +6, poisons −2 |
| 🌑 **New moon** | Poisons +6, elixirs −2 |
| 🌔 **Gibbous moon** | Elixirs +3 |
| 🌒 **Crescent moon** | Poisons +3 |
| 🕛 **Witching hour** (midnight–4am) | +5 to every brew |
| 🌆 **Nightfall** (7pm onward) | +2 |
| 🌅 **Dawn** | Elixirs +3, poisons −2 |
| ☀️ **Daylight** | Poisons −2 |
| 🎃 **All Hallows' Eve** (Oct 31) | +8 to every brew |
| 💀 **Day of the Dead** (Nov 1–2) | +2, or +5 for bone brews |
| 🐈‍⬛ **Friday the 13th** | Poisons +5, elixirs −2 |
| ♨️ **Seasoned pot** | Same essence brewed again in a row: +2 per brew, up to +6 |
| 📖 **Untried recipe** | First time brewing this exact combination: +3 |
| ✨ **The cauldron's whim** | A random twist each brew, from −3 to +5 |

### Rarity
| Rarity | Arcana | Potions |
|---|---|---|
| ⚪ Common | below 28 | 12 |
| 🔵 Rare | 28+ | 8 |
| 🟠 Legendary | 44+ | 6 |
| 🟣 Mythic | 58+ | 3 |
| 🌟 Heavenly | 75+ **and a sign in the sky** | 2 |

A **Heavenly** brew only forms under a sign: a full or new moon, the witching hour, or All Hallows' Eve. Without one, it settles at Mythic. Brews made only of common pantry ingredients always stay Common.

## Lore

### The Apothecary
Nobody remembers who first kept the shop at the end of the lane. The sign has been repainted so many times the name has worn away, but the grimoire on the counter remains: thick, leather-bound, and mostly blank. Each keeper adds what they discover. Each keeper eventually leaves. The cauldron stays lit.

You are its newest keeper.

### The four essences
Every magical ingredient answers to one of four essences. The fifth shelf holds plain pantry goods.

- **♥ Blood** — pulse, hunger and the warmth of living things. *Vampire Fang, Heartbeat Rose, Dragon's Blood Resin.* Blood brews heal hearts or stop them.
- **☠ Bone** — patience, endurance, the long sleep of the grave. *Mandrake Root, Ancient Amber, Graveyard Dirt.* Bone brews make things that last, or things that never move again.
- **✧ Spirit** — breath, memory and what lingers after. *Ghost Breath, Banshee Tear, Moonflower.* Spirit brews speak to dreams and to the dead.
- **☾ Shadow** — secrets, luck and the dark between candles. *Nightshade, Black Cat Whisker, Midnight Lotus.* Shadow brews hide you, or curse those who see you.
- **◦ Common** — honey, salt, spring water, a clove of garlic. No magic of their own, but a wise apothecary knows a pinch of salt can calm a quarrelling cauldron.

### Bonds and clashes
Some ingredients remember each other. The **Vampire Fang** and the **Bat Wing** were once the same creature, and in the pot they hunt together. **Moonflower** and **Midnight Lotus** bloom only when the other is near. **Raven Bone** and **Crow Feather** keep an old pact between birds.

Others never forgave. The fang still shrinks from **Garlic**. **Werewolf Fur** singes at the touch of **Wolfsbane**. And no one has ever successfully mixed **Honey** with **Vinegar**. Eighteen such reactions are hidden in the shelves. The grimoire records each one you find.

### The night sky
The old keepers swore the cauldron listened to the sky, and they were right. A full moon pours silver into elixirs. A new moon gives poisons their teeth. At the witching hour, every brew runs stronger. And on **All Hallows' Eve**, when the veil between worlds is thinnest, the cauldron answers almost anything.

### The cauldron's whim
The cauldron is old and has opinions. Sometimes a crow caws at the window and the brew sours. Sometimes a black cat purrs by the hearth and everything goes just right. Once, the stories say, a shooting star crossed the chimney at the exact moment of brewing. Keepers still argue about what came out of the pot that night.

### The Heavenly potions
Only two potions are written in the grimoire's final pages, and no keeper is known to have brewed both.

- 🌟 ***Seraph's Last Light*** *(Heavenly elixir)* — *Heals every wound the drinker ever had, and a few they have yet to suffer.* Said to glow faintly gold even in a sealed bottle.
- 🌟 ***Nocturne of the Blood Count*** *(Heavenly poison)* — *One drop and the drinker belongs to the night forever.* The bottle is cold to the touch, and those who hold it too long hear a waltz playing somewhere far away.

Both need perfect craft and a sign in the sky. The rest is up to you.

### A few entries from the grimoire
- ***Kitchen Sink Soup*** *(Common)* — No magic at all, just a pot of pantry odds and ends. Every new keeper brews this first.
- ***Jack-o'-Lantern Tea*** *(Common)* — Glows a faint orange and smells of carved pumpkin. Harmless, mostly.
- ***Black Cat Curse*** *(Rare poison)* — Every step finds a puddle. Every ladder falls.
- ***Whisperwind Cordial*** *(Legendary elixir)* — Dreams arrive as clear as memories.
- ***Veil of the Hollow Moon*** *(Mythic)* — Walk unseen through any door for one full night.

*The other 26 are left for you to discover.*

## Tips for new keepers
1. **Brew everything once.** Brewing reveals an ingredient's bane, balm and power on its label.
2. **Pick a side.** Mixing banes and balms causes discord. Commit to an elixir or a poison.
3. **Stack an essence.** Three or more of one essence builds real harmony.
4. **Start with your strongest essence.** A true base is a free +3.
5. **Brew the same essence back to back** to season the pot.
6. **Watch the sky.** Save your best recipes for a full moon (elixirs), a new moon (poisons), or the witching hour.
7. **Try new combinations.** Untried recipes get a bonus, and they're how you find reactions.
