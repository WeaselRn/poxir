      Grimoire

      Reactions found

      Pick five or six ingredients and brew. Your ingredients set the brew's craft. The night adds its omens. Together they decide how rare a potion you bottle.

      How the cauldron judges

      Every ingredient has a hidden bane or balm value and a power. Brewing one reveals its numbers on its label.

      More bane makes a poison, more balm makes an elixir. Ingredients that pull the other way cause discord. Three or more of one essence builds harmony. Some pairs react, for better or worse.

      All of that is the brew's craft. Add the omens and you get its arcana, which sets its rarity.

      Bane and balm

      The cauldron adds up every bane and balm. If bane wins, you brewed a poison. Otherwise it is an elixir. The winning margin, doubled, becomes the brew's potency.

      Power, discord and harmony

      Every ingredient's power is added together. An ingredient that pulls against the brew costs twice its value as discord, and Rock Salt halves the discord.

      The most common essence names the potion and adds harmony: two of a kind give +2, three +5, four +9, five +13, six +16. Commons carry no essence.

      Pairs, commons and the true base

      A bond adds 6, a clash takes 6 away, and Spring Water cancels one clash. Honey adds 4 to an elixir, Vinegar 4 to a poison and Candle Wax 3 to anything.

      The first ingredient you add is the base. If it shares the brew's leading essence, the brew gains 3.

      Omens

      The moon. A full moon adds 6 to elixirs and costs poisons 2. A new moon does the opposite. A gibbous moon adds 3 to elixirs, a crescent adds 3 to poisons.

      The hour. The witching hour (midnight to 4am) adds 5. Nightfall (7pm onward) adds 2. Dawn adds 3 to elixirs and costs poisons 2. Daylight costs poisons 2.

      The date. All Hallows' Eve adds 8. The Day of the Dead adds 2, or 5 to bone brews. Friday the 13th adds 5 to poisons and costs elixirs 2.

      The pot. Brewing the same essence again seasons the pot: +2 for the second brew in a row, up to +6. An untried recipe adds 3. Every brew also gets the cauldron's whim, from −3 to +5.

      Rarity

      A Heavenly brew also needs a sign in the sky: a full or new moon, the witching hour, or All Hallows' Eve. Brews made only of commons stay Common.
